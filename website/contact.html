Make it a one-round trip

What to include

Polishly touches accessibility, the clipboard, and four different providers, so the same symptom can have very different causes. These five lines usually turn a week of back-and-forth into a single reply.

  • Your macOS or Windows version and Polishly version
  • The app you were writing in — Notes, Mail, Slack, Teams, Chrome
  • The provider and model you had selected, or Demo mode
  • What you expected to happen, and what actually happened
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes

Please do not send API keys. No one at Polishly will ever ask for one. If a rewrite of sensitive text misbehaved, describe the shape of the problem rather than pasting the text itself.
